## Part 2: The Five Learning Priority Levels

The 5 priority levels describe the **recommended pedagogical sequence and learning depth**, independent of domain boundaries:

### Level 1 — Core Foundations
- Excel Fundamentals, Formulas & Pivot Tables
- SQL Fundamentals, Filtering, Aggregations & Joins
- Mathematics and Statistics Fundamentals (Descriptive stats, Probability)
- Python Fundamentals (Syntax, Data Structures, Functions)
- NumPy and Pandas
- Data Cleaning Essentials
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Tableau and Power BI Foundations

### Level 2 — Analytical Proficiency
- Business Understanding & Business Models
- Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Data Visualization Principles & Visual Storytelling
- Business Intelligence Reporting Practices
- Written & Verbal Stakeholder Communication
- Data Storytelling & Executive Summaries
- Practical Guided Projects
- Version Control with Git and GitHub

### Level 3 — Professional Differentiation
- A/B Testing & Experimentation
- Cohort Analysis & Retention Dynamics
- Customer Segmentation & RFM
- Advanced SQL (Window functions, CTEs, Query Optimization)
- Advanced Tableau / Power BI (Calculated Fields, DAX, Data Models)
- Advanced Excel (Power Query, Dynamic Arrays, Solver)
- Data Governance, Quality, Lineage & Ethics
- Industry-Specific Analytics (Retail, Finance, Healthcare, Product, Marketing)

### Level 4 — Advanced / Optional
- Machine Learning Fundamentals (Supervised, Unsupervised)
- Predictive Analytics & Time-series Analysis (ARIMA, Forecasting)
- Data Engineering Fundamentals (ETL/ELT, Pipelines)
- Big Data (Spark, PySpark, Hadoop)
- Cloud Data Warehouses (BigQuery, Snowflake, Synapse)
- Generative AI & LLMs for Data Analytics
- AI Agents & Text-to-SQL
- Advanced Inferential Statistics & Non-parametric tests

### Level 5 — Career Preparation
- Resume Building & ATS Optimization
- LinkedIn Optimization & Presence
- Professional Portfolio Development
- Public GitHub Repositories
- Internship & Job Application Strategy
- Professional Networking
- Analytical Aptitude & Problem Solving
- Technical Interviews (SQL, Python, Excel, Stats)
- Business Case Study Interviews
- Mock Interviews & Behavioral Preparation
- Presentation Defense & Critical Thinking
