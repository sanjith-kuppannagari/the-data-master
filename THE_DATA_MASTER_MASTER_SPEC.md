## Personalised Data Learning, Practice & Competition Platform

**Tagline:** Learn. Analyse. Master Data.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# PROJECT OVERVIEW

Build a modern, premium, interactive and gamified data-learning platform
called **THE DATA MASTER**.

The Data Master is a personalised learning ecosystem designed to help
students, professionals, beginners and career switchers develop
practical data-related abilities.

The platform combines the underlying product strengths of three
different experiences:

1.  **Duolingo:** Engagement, motivation, gamification, progression and
    consistency.
2.  **DataCamp:** Structured learning, interactive lessons, practical
    exercises and hands-on coding.
3.  **Kaggle:** Real-world datasets, independent experimentation,
    projects, competitions and community participation.

These are references for functionality and product philosophy ONLY.

Do not copy their branding, visual designs, layouts, colours,
illustrations, navigation or interface patterns.

Create an original product with its own visual identity and experience.

The central idea is:

**Learn → Practise → Challenge → Compete → Build.**

The platform must not feel like a conventional online course website. It
should feel like a premium digital exploration studio where users
actively develop, test and demonstrate their abilities.

The Data Master must not force every user through the same curriculum.

their own requirements.

The original 15-domain syllabus must act as the platform's complete
knowledge library.

Users choose what they want to learn, while the platform recommends
relevant prerequisites and learning sequences.
